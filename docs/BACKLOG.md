# Backlog — Rock Climber: The Ritual

Source of truth. One table per domain section; a row joins exactly one section.

**Status** is one of: `proposed` · `approved` · `parked` · `rejected` · `shipped (vX.Y.Z)`
Shipped rows stay forever, as history. Rejected rows keep their reason.

**IDs** are `B<n>`, immutable, never reused. Take the next one by re-reading the highest number
in this file immediately before writing, never from memory.

**Notes** is where the value is: what is known now, what is proposed, the triage decision with
its date, and after shipping, what was actually built and how it was verified. Rows grow over
their lifetime; nothing is overwritten.

There is no priority column and no estimate column on purpose. Priority is the prose block below.

Tried raising the reach instead and reverted it, because a longer reach made a free hand steer toward the next hold rather than the one under it. |
| B13 | More than one route, or a daily seed | Claude 2026-09-02 | shipped (v0.3.0) | Generation is already deterministic per seed; only seed 7 is ever used. Cheap to expose, but pointless before B1. Shipped v0.3.0: `?seed=` picks the line, and the title screen offers four hand-checked ones -- Ritual (7, the original), Ladder (21: 42 jugs, 5% poor rock down low against 40% up high), Serpent (4: wanders 0.70 m either side of centre), Ordeal (19: 31 crimps, 18 slopers, 68% poor rock up high, 9 decoys). Chosen by screening seeds 1-40 through the playability bot: all 40 topped out with 0 misses, so the four were picked for character rather than validity, and each was then walked in the browser. Choosing one reloads the page with the seed in the URL (keeping the other parameters), because a new route rebuilds the cliff, the decoys and the arms -- and it leaves a link that opens the same climb. An unlisted `?seed=` still generates under the same rules and shows as its own pill. Tests: the bot now climbs every seed on the roster, the roster is checked for duplicates and names, and the parameter parsing is unit-tested. 67 tests to 70. |
| B35 | A decoy that crumbled stays gone after Climb again | Claude 2026-09-02, found while building B11 | shipped (v0.3.1) | `restart()` builds a new route from the same seed, so the sim's decoys are whole again, but world.js captured the boot-time fake objects in `fakeParts` and never re-syncs them. Holds already do this through syncRoute/holdById; decoys do not. After a restart every decoy that fell last climb is still invisible and 60 m below, while the sim will happily crumble it again: you can grab a rock that is not there. The v0.3.0 dust lands in the right place only because the same seed puts the decoy in the same spot. Fix is a fakeById sync in syncRoute plus resetting the fakeParts groups. | Shipped v0.3.1. `syncRoute` now calls a `syncFakes` that rebuilds `fakeById` from the new route and re-points every `fakeParts` entry at the object carrying its id, then puts the group back: position zeroed, spin undone, visible again, fall and vy reset. It hangs off the same route-identity check the holds already used, so it costs one comparison a frame and runs exactly once per restart. The baked geometry is still right because a restart keeps the seed, and picking a different seed reloads the page. Verified in the browser by driving the real `restart()` the Climb again button calls: two decoys crumbled and fell to -18.21 m, after the restart all seven groups were back at y = 0 and visible, and breaking the same two ids on the NEW route made them fall again -- which is the proof the parts are bound to the new objects and not the old ones. Also measured on the pixels, camera parked a metre off decoy 10000: losing the rock changed 36.2% of the centre of the frame and getting it back changed 34.7%, while the intact frame before and the intact frame after differed by 18% against a 23.9% noise floor measured between two intact frames over the same elapsed time -- i.e. below the noise. Console clean throughout. No test guards it: world.js needs WebGL and `node --test` is the pure-logic suite. |
