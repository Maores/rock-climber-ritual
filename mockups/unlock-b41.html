B41 — how you unlock ARACHNID on a phone

The code is matched by a keydown listener on the title screen. An iPhone has no keyboard there and nothing summons one.

So on your phone the ✦ button never appears, the glove is never worn, and the whole web-zip — B14 to B21, all of v0.2.0 — is dead code. You asked for it and have never been able to reach it.

A · The sigil becomes a web of letters my pick

Hold the sigil for about a second. The alphabet arranges itself around the ring and you tap out the word; the letters you have found fill in beneath. Wrong letter and the ring quietly resets.

The word survives — the egg is still a code you have to know, not a button. It looks like the game rather than like a form, and it reads as deliberately hidden, which is what an easter egg is for. Desktop keeps typing exactly as it does now.

Cost: the most to build — a letter ring, its hit targets and its reset.

B · Eight taps on the sigil

Eight legs, eight taps. Nothing on screen counts them until the fifth, when faint dots appear so you know something is happening.

Cheapest to build by a distance, and impossible to fumble on a phone.

Cost: the word is gone. ARACHNID stops being the secret and the egg becomes a button you happen to press a lot — and it can be tripped by accident.

C · Tap the sigil to summon the real keyboard

A hidden text field takes focus, iOS raises its own keyboard, and you type ARACHNID exactly as you would on a desktop.

Smallest change, and one truth for both platforms: the code is typed, full stop. Nothing new to design.

Cost: the system keyboard slides up over the title screen, which looks like a bug report form rather than a ritual — and nothing tells you the sigil is tappable.

All three keep the desktop path unchanged. Pick one and I will build it.
