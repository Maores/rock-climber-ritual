WEB-ZIP — THE REMAINING CALLS

Locked in already: a secret code unlocks a Spider-Man right hand. It shoots a web from the 🤘 pose, pulls you to whatever it hits, and never lets go of the rock or tires.

✔ web-zip  ·  ✔ 🤘 activation  ·  ✔ pull yourself in  ·  ✔ sticky + endless grip

1 · The gesture How does the pose fire the web?

✓

Hold the pose to aim Recommended

Holding the grip button curls the hand into 🤘 and a web line follows your aim. Let go and it fires. You see the shot before you commit, like winding up.

✓

Fire instantly

Double-tap the grip and the web goes straight to the best target above you. Fastest and flashiest; you get less say in where it lands.

2 · Aiming Where does the web actually go?

✓

Free aim, anywhere Recommended

The web goes wherever you point, hold or not. With sticky hands the bare wall is a valid landing, so the whole cliff opens up. Most Spider-Man, most freedom.

✓

Snap to holds

Aim roughly and the web takes the nearest hold. Forgiving on a phone thumb, but the cliff still behaves like a ladder.

✓

Fire where you look

Reuses the look-around you just got: turn your head, fire along your gaze. Elegant and cheap to build, but you cannot aim and watch your body at once.

3 · The glove How literal should it be?

✓

Classic red and blue Recommended

Unmistakable. Note it is Marvel's costume design, so it stays a personal fan Easter egg behind a secret code, never advertised on the page.

✓

Stealth, teal webbing

Same silhouette and webbing, but dark with the game's own teal glow. Reads as Spider-Man to anyone who knows, and belongs to this cliff at dusk.

✓

The ritual's own web

No costume at all: your same chalked hand, with a glowing web sigil in the palm. Fits the fiction, and the power is the surprise rather than the outfit.

4 · The code How do you unlock it?

✓

Type it on the title screen Recommended

No visible box: start typing and letters appear. The right word flashes the rune and unlocks the hand for good. Works on the phone too, via a hidden field.

✓

A secret sequence

Old-school cheat code. Fun on a keyboard, awkward on a phone unless it maps to the grip buttons.

✓

A hidden rune on the cliff

No typing at all: one secret rune sits off the line. Reach it and hold it. Most in-world, and the hardest to stumble on by accident.

Whatever you pick, the egg stays behind the code: nothing on the title screen, the store page or the repo advertises it, and the credits stay as they are.
